## Description
A creator workspace with Xiaohongshu and Douyin pipelines for MIEMIE Travel. Every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday it ranks the top three New Zealand tourism, charter, and guide posts, deconstructs why they went viral, and stores the formulas for second-creation drafts aimed at travellers who need a charter or guide. Xiaohongshu is paused for now until Kin gives the go-ahead.
