# Sleep apnea self-test week

Goal ID: goal_07267931d9f3
Goal slug: sleep-apnea-self-test-week

## Description
A seven-night self-monitoring run (Oct 23–29) to assess whether the snoring may involve sleep apnea: nightly snore-app recordings, spouse observations for breathing pauses, and a morning code on dry mouth, bitter taste, headache, and night waking. Muse logs the daily reports and consolidates the results on October 30 morning, then recommends next steps such as a pulse-oximetry night or a GP visit.
