## Description
Kin will move from Auckland to Christchurch at the end of December. He will first take a furnished en-suite room in a shared house for about three months to bypass the Christmas and New Year shutdown. He will then search for a long-term whole rental, furnish it, connect water, power and internet, and arrange onward letting.
